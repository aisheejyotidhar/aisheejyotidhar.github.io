About

Engineering shaped by fieldwork as much as coursework.

I recently completed my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology, with a particular interest in sustainable infrastructure, water resource engineering, and structural design. My academic path has run alongside real exposure to how infrastructure actually gets built — from soil testing and structural analysis to GIS-based planning and a field visit to an active bridge project.

That mix shows up in my work: an industrial attachment at a government engineering department, a landscape design competition entry for my university's central pond, and two co-authored research papers on air quality across Bangladesh's major cities. I've also completed a leadership development program and volunteered with my university's career forum — pairing technical grounding with the collaboration and communication skills that infrastructure work depends on.

I'm now looking for opportunities in infrastructure design, consulting, or public-sector engineering where I can keep building on that foundation.
